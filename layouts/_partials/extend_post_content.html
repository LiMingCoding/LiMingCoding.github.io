Caveat: if *some* posts in a series have weight and others don't, the unweighted ones all sit at weight=0 and will appear first. To get a predictable order, set weight on every post in the series. We also render a "前一篇 / 下一篇" navigation row below the list so readers can step through the series without bouncing back to the landing page.
